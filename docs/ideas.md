# Ideas for you

Bigger ideas that need your go-ahead (they cost money, need an account, or
change how you use Gear Scout). Smaller improvements just get done.

- **Late-night eBay auctions** — research shows auctions ending between midnight and 6 AM get fewer bids. Gear Scout could flag ShopGoodwill/eBay auctions ending overnight with a current bid well under the comp, and push you an hour before they end so you can set a max bid. (No account needed for the alert; bidding stays with you.)
- **Saved-search links for sites Gear Scout can't read** — one-tap buttons on the Telex page that open each term (and its common misspellings) on Facebook Marketplace, eBay "sold" and Reverb in your own browser, so you can check by hand in seconds.
- **Price-drop watch** — ads that drop their price twice are usually motivated sellers; a "dropping" badge plus a push when a watched model drops below the comp.
- **Estate / auction houses** — local estate-sale and auction aggregators (e.g. estatesales.net, HiBid, LiveAuctioneers) often have old broadcast and studio gear sold by people who don't know its value. Some need a free account to see prices, which only you can create.
