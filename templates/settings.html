Email digest

The email is capped so it doesn't grow large enough that some email clients (Gmail included) truncate it — anything past the cap is still visible on the Dashboard, just not spelled out in the email itself. Raising these a lot will make for a bigger email; keep an eye out if it starts looking cut off in your inbox.

Whatever you'd actually type into a browser to reach this dashboard from wherever you read your email — http://localhost:8420 only works if you're opening the email on this same machine. Use your LAN IP (e.g. http://192.168.1.20:8420) or your ngrok URL if you read email on your phone.

Schedule

Cron format is minute hour day month weekday. The hour field takes a comma-separated list, so 0 7,12,17,21 * * * runs at 7am, noon, 5pm, and 9pm (in the timezone above) — leave minute at 0, day/month/weekday at *, unless you actually need something more specific. Find your timezone name at this list.

Timezone/cron changes need a restart — the scheduler reads these once when the scout container starts, not on every run, so a change here won't take effect until you run docker compose restart scout. Everything else on this tab doesn't need that — it's re-read fresh on every run.

Your location

Your ZIP code, used only to show how far Facebook Marketplace and Craigslist listings are from you and to filter by distance. Stays on this machine.

Exclude words

One per line. Any listing whose title contains one of these is dropped everywhere — scheduled runs, live search, the Dashboard and the email. Good for things like wanted, wtb or looking for. Broken gear isn't hidden — it's tagged Needs repair instead.

Reset everything

Deletes all stored listings, favorites, and tags — every source will look "new" again on the next scrape. Doesn't touch config.yaml (your sources/keywords/email settings are untouched) or your saved Facebook session.

Facebook post search — priority list

Terms searched in Facebook's search bar (Posts, newest first) on every scheduled scrape, grouped by category. Put each category in square brackets, then one term per line under it. Each term takes about 30 seconds and is a search on your Facebook account, so keep it to your most important 15–20 terms (at most 40 run each time). Results show on the FB Posts page, on the Dashboard and in the email.

Phone notifications

Instant alerts on your phone for new matches on your saved searches and price drops on your favorites, using the free ntfy app — no account needed.

  1. Install ntfy from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. In the app tap + (Subscribe to topic) and enter the channel name below exactly.
  3. Click Save alert settings here, then Send test notification.

Anyone who knows the channel name can see these alerts, so keep the random one suggested below rather than something guessable.

Each check searches every site (including 8 Facebook regions) once per saved search, so very short intervals with many saved searches mean a lot of activity on your Facebook account. Changing the interval takes effect after docker compose restart scout.

eBay results

{% if ebay_client_id and ebay_has_secret %} Connected eBay listings (used, open box, seller refurbished) come in through eBay's official search service, in scheduled runs and live searches. {% else %} Not set up eBay blocks scraping, so until this is set up eBay shows as "manual check only". {% endif %}

eBay's official search service is free but needs your own keys. One-time setup:

  1. Go to developer.ebay.com, sign in with your eBay account and join the (free) Developers Program. Approval can take up to a day.
  2. Open Application Keys and create a Production keyset (not Sandbox) — any app name works.
  3. If eBay asks about "marketplace account deletion" notifications before enabling the keyset, answer that question for yourself — Gear Scout only stores listings (title, price, link), never eBay users' account details.
  4. Copy the Production App ID (Client ID) and Cert ID (Client Secret) into the fields below.
