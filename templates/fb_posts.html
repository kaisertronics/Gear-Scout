📣 Facebook post search

Searches Facebook's site-wide search bar (Posts, newest first) — public posts and posts in groups you're in — using your saved Facebook login. It works through your whole search-term list in the background all day (priority terms come around several times a day) and emails one roundup of what it found each afternoon; search anything once with the box. Only posts from the last 14 days are kept — Gear Scout reads each post's real date (Facebook's own "recent" sort still mixes in old posts, and posts whose date can't be read are skipped). Posts with a photo link straight to the post; text-only ones link to the search they came from.
