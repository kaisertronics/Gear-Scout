💰 Saved

What you saved on your gear vs. buying it new, how you did against the used market, and where you overpaid. Keep a Google Sheet with one row per item: column A = the gear, column B = what you paid. No headers needed. Put a new price in column C only if you want to override the lookup. Each piece gets today's new price (Reverb Brand New + eBay New) and its going used price (your Telex List prices, then listings Gear Scout has found, then Reverb). When no new price can be found (vintage gear, or gear sold only direct by its maker) it's compared to the used price. "x 2", "pair" or a plural name counts two; split a row with " / " to price two different things. A price in column C always wins. In Sheets choose Share → Anyone with the link → Viewer, then paste the link here.
