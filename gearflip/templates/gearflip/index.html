How the numbers work
  • All-in buy: the asking price + their shipping + sales tax ({{ st.buy_tax_pct }}%) when you buy online, or + {{ st.local_pickup_cost|gf_money }} for a local pickup trip (no tax on private sales).
  • Sell for: what this model really goes for used, from Gear Scout's year of prices (real Reverb sales when there are 3+, otherwise asking prices trimmed 10%, since gear sells below its ask). Condition words (mint, rough) are matched.
  • Where to sell: whichever earns the most after a small cost for money tied up. {% for k, c in channels.items() %}{{ c.label }}: {{ (c.pct * 100)|round(1) }}% fees{% if c.fixed %} + ${{ c.fixed }}{% endif %}{% if c.price != 1 %}, sells ~{{ ((1 - c.price) * 100)|round|int }}% lower{% endif %}{{ '; ' if not loop.last else '.' }}{% endfor %}
  • Shipping: by size (mic box $28, rack unit $48, monitors/synths $70, consoles and tape machines $160) plus 1% insurance.
  • Repairs: typical bench cost and the odds the fix works: {% for k, r in repairs.items() %}{{ k }} ${{ r[0] }} ({{ (r[1] * 100)|int }}%){{ ', ' if not loop.last else '.' }}{% endfor %} If it can't be fixed you still sell it for parts (about 30% of its value). Net profit is the average of the two outcomes; "worst realistic case" is the bad one.
  • Picture analysis: with your free Gemini key, an AI looks at each strong flip's photo. A box, case, accessory or different model is hidden. Worn or damaged gear gets a lower resale price (damage on "working" gear adds a check-over), and missing parts lower it too. A stock photo lowers confidence; a real photo of the right unit raises it. Pushes and the plan wait for this check.
  • Not the real thing: ads for just the box or manual, look-alikes ("C414 style", "like a U87", "vs", "clone") and prices far under value are left out before any math.
  • Confidence: more comparable ads, real sales, low-risk repairs and believable prices raise it; prices far under value and warning signs lower it.
  • Score: profit, ROI and speed, scaled by confidence. Walk-away price: the most you can pay and still hit your {{ st.min_roi }}% / {{ st.min_profit|gf_money }} minimums. Use it for offers.
  • Best buys: the set that makes the most confidence-weighted profit with the cash you have free (budget + profits − money in unsold flips).
